# External audit (19 Oct 2026): verdict per finding

Source: "Waypoint Engine evaluation and implementation report" (Codex, 19 Oct 2026, 25 pages), reviewed at commit 61787d1.
Checked against the same commit and the live Contacts DB on 20 Oct. This page records what we accept, what we re-rate and why. The roadmap (docs/roadmap.md) carries the resulting work.

## Bottom line
- The code findings are accurate. F2, F3, F6, the unconditional "nothing was sent" alert, no backups and unpinned images were all confirmed in the generators.
- The severities assume a multi-tenant, auto-sending product. Today the engine is one operator, every card is a human tap, and every cold lead becomes a Gmail **draft**. Several "Critical/High" items are Low in that mode and become High only when auto-send, follow-ups (WP · 60) or a client deployment arrive.
- The audit's repair plan sums to roughly 28–50 engineering days. The engine is not the bottleneck: on 20 Oct, 12 Gmail drafts created since 16 Oct have no recorded send. Selling time beats hardening time.
- Two points the audit understates: the legal exposure for German recipients, and an SSRF path to the Hetzner metadata service (see below).

## Where the audit is understated
1. **German recipients.** UWG §7(2) Nr. 2 needs prior express consent for advertising e-mail, B2B included; the "presumed consent" carve-out is for phone calls to businesses. Sending the Gmail draft by hand does not change that. Many current targets are outside Germany (FI, FR, BE), where B2B rules differ, but the ICP says "entering DACH". Practical rule until advised otherwise: German/Austrian recipients → LinkedIn or warm intro, not cold e-mail. This also matters for selling "GDPR-aware automation" (offer 5). Not legal advice: one hour with a lawyer before the engine is sold to a client.
2. **Metadata SSRF.** `infra/cloud-init.yaml` pastes the Cloudflare tunnel token into server user data, which the Hetzner metadata service (169.254.169.254) returns to any local request. Site scan and homepage checks follow prospect-controlled URLs. A redirect to that address could pull the token into fetched text. Fix: firewall 169.254.169.254 from the Docker network (DOCKER-USER chain) and enable `N8N_SSRF_PROTECTION_ENABLED` once on a supporting n8n version; rotate the tunnel token if the server was built from cloud-init.

## Where the audit overreaches
- Application database, dispatcher, state machine, staging stack, CI, autonomy levels 0–4, tenant isolation: correct designs for a product with customers. None is needed to win the next Waypoint client.
- Cost model: €299/month SaaS at 5 customers = 7.6% contribution is the audit's own evidence that the near-term business is service, not software. We agree and go further: SaaS is off the roadmap until a client pays for an engine deployment.
